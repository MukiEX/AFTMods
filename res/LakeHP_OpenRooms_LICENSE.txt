LakeHP_OpenRooms.dll: open room filters for DivaLinux's headphone surround ([audio] in
DivaLinux.ini), from LakeHP (github.com/MukiEX/LakeHeadphones, filters/).

It holds three rooms (Open Small / Medium / Large) at 48 and 44.1 kHz, built by LakeHP's
tools/make_open_filters.py from subject p0010 of the SONICOM HRTF dataset (Imperial College
London, Audio Experience Design group), as redistributed with the DAFx 2026 HRTF tutorial
(github.com/yzyouzhang/dafx2026-hrtf-tutorial). The DLL holds data only, no code.
